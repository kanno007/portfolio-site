From phone calls and paper to one request everyone can see

EuroPeir collects industrial waste in Italy with hook-lift trucks and roll-off containers. Every job used to start as an email, a call or a WhatsApp message, get typed in again by dispatch, and leave the depot as a sheet of paper. I designed the Hub that replaces that routine: a customer portal, a dispatch console with a management dashboard, and a driver app, all working from the same request.
