    Context

    Codes are written to be complete, not to be read in the sun with one free hand.

    The existing mobile experience treated them as documents to scroll. People on site treat them as questions to answer.

    How I found out

    Research before commitment.

    Framed around the three jobs people on site actually have: find the right section fast, read it without losing the hierarchy, and get back to it later. Every screen was judged against those three, not against the document structure.

    What I did

    Finding, reading and returning.

    Redesigned the mobile experience around three jobs: search that understands code references, a reading view that keeps hierarchy visible in long sections, and navigation that remembers where you were. Dense reference material became something you could use standing up.

    What changed

    Usable where it’s actually needed.

    The code became searchable and usable on site, not only at a desk.
