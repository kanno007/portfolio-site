The first 90 days as the only designer

What I would do again from my first three months at Novo, and the one thing I would skip.

In this post
    Share

    When I joined Novo there was no design team, no files and no system. There was a product idea, a few engineers in New York and a bank partner. Everything I know about starting from zero comes from that first quarter.

    Weeks one to four: listen before drawing

    I spent the first month talking to small business owners, the support inbox and the engineers. Not to produce a research deck. To find out which part of opening and running an account people dreaded. It was onboarding, by a mile, and that told me where the first real screens should be.

    Weeks five to eight: one flow, finished

    I picked onboarding and took it all the way to shipped. One flow done properly teaches the team what working with a designer looks like, and it gives you a reference everyone can point at when the next flow comes up.

    Weeks nine to twelve: start the system, small

    By then I had drawn the same button four times. That is the signal. I made a small library of the things I kept repeating and wrote one page on how to use it. It grew into the system that later ran across five surfaces, but it started as a way to stop myself from drifting.

    The thing I would skip: the big vision deck I made in week two. Nobody needed it. The finished onboarding flow did its job better.
