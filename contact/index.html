How it usually goes

No deck and no discovery theatre. Three steps, and you can stop after any of them.

01

A thirty-minute call

You describe the product and what is slowing it down. I ask about the flow, the numbers you already have, and who owns what. I tell you honestly whether I am the right person for it, and if not, who might be.

02

A short written take

Within a few days: what I would change first, what I would leave alone, and roughly what it would take. Written so you can forward it to whoever else needs to decide.

03

A scoped first piece of work

One flow end to end, a UX audit, or the foundations of a design system. Small enough to judge the fit, real enough to ship.
