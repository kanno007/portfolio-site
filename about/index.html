How I work with a business

I ask for the whole workflow, not the screen. Then I solve it with design, with AI, and with the team around me.

The whole workflow

A screen is never the problem. The flow it sits in is.

A KYC form that loses customers is a revenue problem. A prescription queue that runs on phone calls is an operations problem. A subscription that quietly lapses is a retention problem. I take ownership of that whole flow, from the business decision to the shipped product, and I use design and AI to fix it rather than decorate it. That is why my work at Novo included landing pages and activation funnels, and why at AesthetiDocs I help the founders decide what to build at all.

From zero

The job changes from drawing every screen to making sure every screen is worth drawing.

At Novo I was hired as the only designer and left having built the team, the product and the system they ran on. Along the way the role moved from execution to critique, mentoring, planning with product and setting company-wide OKRs, without ever leaving the file.

In the room

Most of design leadership is conversation.

At Infobeans I ran client workshops and reviewed work before it reached clients. At AesthetiDocs I work directly with the founders, presenting trade-offs in plain business terms so they can decide what ships. The design is only as good as the decision it helps someone else make.

Still designing

Written, teachable feedback on real screens.

I review work in writing, on the frame, and explain the principle rather than fix the pixel. If the same note stops coming up after a month, that part of the job is done and the team needs me for harder calls instead.

Habits that survive every project

Four things I do regardless of the company, the domain or the tooling.

01

Hypothesis before pixels

Write down what we think is true and how we would know. Then design. Opinions are cheaper to change before they are rendered.

02

Feedback in writing

On the frame, with the principle named. It stays, it gets reread, and it teaches the next ten screens.

03

Tokens in code first

A token that exists only in Figma is a suggestion. The day it compiles, it becomes a rule both sides can rely on.

04

One hand in the code

HTML and CSS since 2014, Claude Code and Figma MCP since they existed. Close enough to the build to know what my decisions cost.
